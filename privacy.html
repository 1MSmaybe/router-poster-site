What the app does

Router Poster uploads videos created by the developer to the developer's own TikTok account using TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API.

Data we access

When the account owner authorizes the app, it receives an access token and basic profile information (display name) from TikTok. It uses the token only to upload and publish videos the owner has chosen.

Data we store

Access and refresh tokens are kept in a private, access-controlled secret store belonging to the developer. We do not collect, sell, share or publish personal data, and we do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when the owner revokes access (TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services) or on request.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect their data.
